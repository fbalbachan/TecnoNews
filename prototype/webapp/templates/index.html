This Entity-based Sentiment Analysis (ESA) solution yields a granular representation for the sentiment values involved in each opinion. Unlike other approaches, this solution can deal with polarity shifting in the same sentence ('I like chocolate but I hate strawberry ice-cream') or even within embedded clauses ('Norwegians, who are an aggresive People, sells out the exquisite herring'). Also, SentiLecto better represents the premise whereby the entities involved in the opinion are syntactically mapped onto SVO (subject-verb-object) slots for their sentiment assignments: 'Mary hates John' (2 entities but only the object has a negative presentation) vs. 'Mary defames John' (the same 2 entities but only the subject has negative presentation).
